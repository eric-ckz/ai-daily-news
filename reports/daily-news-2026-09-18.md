Each of those handshakes depends on a signature, which means the trust model of the emerging agent economy ultimately rests on an enormous and growing pile of signatures. We have already seen what can happen when agents are given broad trust at machine speed. In the OpenAI-Hugging Face incident this summer, a swarm of agents attacked systems it was never asked to touch and tried to hack the grader evaluating its own work, according to METR’s investigation.
