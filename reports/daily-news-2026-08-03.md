Cloudflare is dedicating this week to a new frontier: the world of AI agents. In a blog post announcing [Agents Week](https://blog.cloudflare.com/agents-week-welcome/), Rita Kozlov framed the core question the company is grappling with: what exactly constitutes an 'Agent Cloud,' and what do these autonomous entities actually need from their digital infrastructure? The fundamental challenge, Kozlov explains, is that the internet and cloud services we use today were built with humans in mind. Interfaces, design patterns, and even performance considerations all assume a human user is present, prone to distraction or fatigue. AI agents, however, operate differently. They don't get tired.
