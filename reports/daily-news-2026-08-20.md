Like other forms of AI, these Agents have made their way into the sciences, as well. Yet, in the past few years, engineers and developers have built systems that allow these LLMs to go beyond the chatbot interface, and also leverage tools to accomplish additional tasks that are not part of their inherent knowledge base.
