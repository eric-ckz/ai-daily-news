[Add Now](https://www.google.com/preferences/source?hl=en&q=https%3A%2F%2Ftech-insider.org%2F) ## What Is a Diffusion LLM, and Why Does It Change the Speed Math? Every mainstream chatbot you have used, from GPT-6 Astra to Claude to Gemini’s Flash and Pro lines, is autoregressive. It predicts the next token, appends it, then predicts the next one after that, one at a time, left to right. That is why streaming responses appear to type themselves out: the model genuinely is generating them in that order. It is also why latency scales with output length. A 2,000-token answer takes roughly twice as long as a 1,000-token one, no matter how fast the underlying hardware is, because each token depends on every token before it. A diffusion LLM works the way an image diffusion model works. It starts with a masked or noisy version of the full output and refines the entire sequence over a small number of parallel steps, gradually replacing noise with real tokens across the whole answer at once.
