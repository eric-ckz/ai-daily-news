Agentic AI works differently, because it can act on your behalf. Take something as ordinary as booking a haircut. A search engine gives you a long list of local hairdressers to work through yourself. You might have an AI assistant that can generate content, but it’s a bit like a chatbot and can’t take actions. The ‘pure’ definition of an agent is a software programme that can execute actions within its environment, subject to predefined constraints. Agentic AI works differently, because it can act on your behalf. Take something as ordinary as booking a haircut. A search engine gives you a long list of local hairdressers to work through yourself. It’s also important to know that not all agents are created equal. There are different types – or tiers – of agents. They might differ in how independently they can operate, the permissions they have, their ability to interact with their environment, and the complexity of what they are being asked to do. You might have an AI assistant that can generate content, but it’s a bit like a chatbot and can’t take actions. The ‘pure’ definition of an agent is a software programme that can execute actions within its environment, subject to predefined constraints. Autonomous or multi-agent systems introduce a new level of risk. These can pursue goals with very significant independence and authority. **Peter Lee, partner and global head of AI governance, Simmons & Simmons.** ## How might agentic AI be used in legal services?
