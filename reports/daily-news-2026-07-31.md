The companies stated their approach is intended to reduce the integration burden by providing ready access to AI environments on top of the underlying infrastructure. ### Industry Shift Towards Platforms Haseeb Budhani, CEO and Co-Founder of Rafay Systems, indicated that the AI infrastructure market is moving beyond simple hardware deployment. He emphasised the need to transform infrastructure into governed, self-service platforms that developers can use and operators can manage at scale. Rafay's role in the project includes simplifying infrastructure bring-up and automating lifecycle management, ensuring developers get immediate access to production-ready AI environments while preserving governance controls for operators. ### Focus on Utilisation The platform is designed to support model development, training, inference, and future AI service delivery. Aolani, founded in 2023, focuses on AI cloud infrastructure in Asia, while Rafay is part of NVIDIA Inception, a programme for start-ups.
