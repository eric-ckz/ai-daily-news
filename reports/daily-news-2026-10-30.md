Image by Tim Sandle Artificial intelligence has already changed how many people research a holiday or business trip. The next change could be considerably more disruptive. Rather than simply suggesting a destination, comparing hotels or constructing an itinerary, AI agents are beginning to move closer to carrying out tasks for travellers. This creates a very different technological challenge: an AI system that understands what somebody wants still needs access to the reservation, payment, identity and entitlement systems required to make it happen. The development potentially changes where competitive advantage lies in digital travel. Companies have spent years encouraging consumers to download individual apps and remain within their proprietary ecosystems. An agentic model could instead place a traveller’s preferred AI assistant between the consumer and the travel company.
